Questions

Good to know before you open a room.

Do I need an account?

No. Pick a display name, share the room link, and you are in. That is the whole signup.

Is my call private?

Voice, video, and screen sharing travel peer-to-peer with WebRTC encryption. Chat is stored on the server you run, so it is not end-to-end encrypted.

Why is my ping high?

Ping is the round-trip time to the server. High values usually mean a weak Wi-Fi signal, a VPN, or distance from the server. The Connection card shows average and jitter to help you tell.

Can I change the theme and text size?

Yes — the theme toggles between light, dark, and automatic (system), and the People panel lets you resize text, compact the chat, and set speaker volume. Everything is saved in your browser.

Quiet by design

Your call is between browsers.

Voice, video, and screen share travel directly between participants using WebRTC. Messages and files stay on the server that runs this room — not in a third-party cloud.

Media is encrypted in transit. Chat is not end-to-end encrypted, so keep truly sensitive conversations out of it. Anyone with the room link can join.
